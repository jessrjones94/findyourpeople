Pilot Group

A first-of-its-kind social experience, designed with neurodivergent needs in mind

First Round: 4–6 People

Small, curated, and intentionally designed for comfort and connection

What the Pilot Is

The pilot group is a small first-round social experience designed to help participants connect in a way that feels calm, clear, and low-pressure. Think guided hangouts with light structure — not a therapy group, not a class, not a networking event.

Every detail has been thought through: the size of the group, the environment, the flow of the session, the activities, and the built-in flexibility for people who need to step away.

What to Expect

4–6 people max
Small, intimate group
Clear structure
Predictable start, flow, and end
Shared activities
Not forced conversation — things to do together
Low-stimulation
Calm, quiet, sensory-aware space
Optional participation
Never pressured to share or talk
Step away option
Breaks always available

Who It's a Good Fit For

This pilot is best suited for people who:

  • Are neurodivergent (autistic, AuDHD, socially anxious, etc.)
  • Want connection but find typical social settings overwhelming
  • Prefer small groups over large gatherings
  • Do well with structure and clear expectations
  • Are independent or semi-independent in a group setting
  • Are respectful of others in the group

What Participants Will NOT Be Expected To Do

  • Perform socially or "act normal"
  • Talk constantly or fill silence
  • Share more than they're comfortable with
  • Jump straight into dating scenarios
  • Stay engaged every second — breaks are always okay
  • Mask or pretend to be someone they're not

What This Is NOT

  • Therapy or counseling
  • Medical or clinical services
  • A high-support care program
  • A dating-only group (friendship and connection come first)
  • A chaotic or unstructured meetup

First Pilot Goals

  • Create genuine comfort and safety
  • Reduce isolation and social burnout
  • Build trust within the group
  • Support natural, authentic connection
  • Learn what format works best for future groups

What Happens After the Pilot?

Based on what we learn from the first cohort, we'll refine the format and potentially offer:

  • Recurring social groups
  • Themed or activity-based groups
  • Friend-making mixers
  • Social confidence workshops
  • Future dating-readiness offerings (if there's interest)
